요리조리(Yorijori)는 음식점·카페 자영업자에게 촬영 레퍼런스를 보여 주는 가이드를 만드는 개인 프로젝트입니다. 누구나 볼 수 있는 공개 정보만 받아 내부 분석에 쓰고, 판매하거나 다른 곳에 넘기지 않습니다. 삭제를 요청하시면 48시간 안에 가이드에서 숨기고 저장된 데이터를 지웁니다.

Yorijori Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · Operator: Yorijori operator · Contact Hexagonist11@gmail.com

Yorijori is a personal project that builds a content reference guide for small restaurant and cafe owners in Korea. We collect only publicly available information, use it for internal analysis, and never sell or share it. If you ask us to delete your data, we hide it from the guide and delete it within 48 hours.

1. What we collect

  • Public Instagram professional (business/creator) accounts, through Meta's official Instagram Graph API (Business Discovery): account name and username, bio, website, follower and media counts, and for public posts the caption, timestamp, media type, like/comment/view counts, permalink and the image or video file.
  • Some public Instagram posts, through a third-party service that collects public data without logging in: public comments (up to 30 per post), paid-partnership labels, and posts of public personal accounts run by stores.
  • Public store information from Naver Place, blogs and the Facebook Ad Library, collected without logging in: store name, location, category, menu, photos, review and blog text, ad activity.
  • Guide users (after launch): anonymous usage events, and the link and optional contact you give when requesting deletion.

We do not collect private accounts, anything that requires logging in, or age-restricted accounts. Review and comment authors are stored only as irreversible hashes, never as nicknames.

2. How we use it

  • To analyze how filming choices relate to engagement and build a reference guide for store owners.
  • We classify images, videos and text with AI services (Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, OpenAI and similar) with model-training data sharing turned off.
  • We do not train AI models on the data or on our analysis results.

3. Display and sharing

  • The guide never republishes original files. Instagram posts are shown only through Instagram's official embed, and Naver content only as links.
  • We do not sell the data or give it to third parties for advertising or other purposes, except when required by law.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Public account and store data, original files and analysis results are kept while the guide runs and monthly changes are compared.
  • We delete the originals and anything derived from them when the account owner, the store or Meta asks, when we learn an account became private or was deleted, or when the service ends.
  • Contact details left with a deletion request are kept up to one year after we handle it, or deleted right away on request.

5. Processors and storage

Supabase (database, Seoul region), Cloudflare R2 (file storage), Anthropic, Google and OpenAI (AI classification), Apify (part of public data collection), and the operator's work computer.

6. Deletion and access requests

Email Hexagonist11@gmail.com. See Data deletion instructions. We reply after hiding and deleting the data within 48 hours. You can also ask what we hold, or ask us to correct it or stop processing it.

7. Changes

We post any change on this page with a new effective date.
